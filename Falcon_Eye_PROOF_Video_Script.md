# Falcon Eye — PROOF Aviation Futures Video Script
**Target: 85 seconds spoken (144 words) + title/end cards ≈ fits the 60–90s window**

Word count is deliberately near the top of the 130–150 band since Act 3 — your
strongest evidence — needs the most room. Cut from Act 4 first if you're over on
time; it's the least evidence-dense act.

## ACT 1 — THE PROBLEM [0:03–0:16]
**VISUAL:** Show, don't tell. Fog rolling across a runway or an aircraft window
whiting out — stock footage or an AI clip (Pika/Runway/Kling) is fine here since
it's illustrative, not evidence. No captions/labels needed yet — this is scene-setting.

**VO (27 words):**

> and pilots are often left with no independent way to see through it."

## ACT 2 — THE SOLUTION [0:16–0:36]
**VISUAL:** Cut to your deck's Slide 3 (the Sense→Detect→Track→Triage→Warn

## ACT 3 — HOW IT WORKS + PROOF [0:36–1:06] — most important 30 seconds
**VISUAL, in order:**
1. Quick pipeline walk-through over the Slide 3 graphic (2-3 sec)
2. Cut to **`outputs/figures/detection_rate_comparison.png`** (the fog-density bar chart) — this is your single strongest piece of evidence, don't rush past it
3. Cut to **`outputs/figures/multi_target_triage.png`** (the triage/reflex figure)

**ON-SCREEN LABEL (required — burn this into the video, don't skip it):**
> **[SIMULATION — 2,400 Monte Carlo trials]**

Put this label on screen the moment the chart appears and leave it up for the
whole chart shot. This is the "honest labels build trust" instruction from the
guide — your results are real simulation output, not a mockup, but they are
simulation, not flight data, and the video needs to say so on screen, not just
in narration.

**VO (50 words):**
> "Falcon Eye senses with LiDAR and radar, tracks every target in real time,
> and triages the most urgent one first with a reflex fast-path. Across 2,400
> Monte Carlo trials and eight tested failure modes, LiDAR alone detected
> nothing in dense fog — fusion held one hundred percent detection, every
> single time."

## ACT 4 — EXPERIENCE + WHAT'S NEXT [1:06–1:31]
**VISUAL:** A simple before/after split — left side: a cockpit view alone in
fog (reuse Act 1's footage); right side: your triage figure or a warning-state
mockup. Then cut to a still image or map of a university campus/UAS test range
(generic/stock is fine — this hasn't happened yet).

**ON-SCREEN LABEL (required, same honesty principle as Act 3):**
> **[CONCEPT — proposed first pilot site]**

This matters: you have not secured a test-range partner yet (your outreach is
in progress). Labeling it as a proposed concept, not a done deal, is exactly
the kind of honesty PROOF's judging criteria rewards over a confident-sounding
overstatement.

**VO (35 words):**
> "Before Falcon Eye: a pilot alone in the fog. After: an independent system
> that still sees the threat coming. Our first stop: a university UAS test
> range, validating real sensor data before real flight."

## Suggested free tools for this specific script
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs or CapCut's built-in TTS — either is fine for a
  research-demo tone; you don't need a dramatic voice here, clear and
  confident reads better than cinematic for a PROOF-judged video
- **Act 1 fog footage:** a short Pika/Runway/Kling clip, or free stock footage
  (Pexels/Pixabay) of fog over a runway — either is legitimate since it's
  illustrative B-roll, not a claim about your system
- **Act 3 screen capture:** you don't need to *record* anything live — just
  bring the two PNG files above into your editor as static images with a slow
  zoom (Ken Burns effect), which reads as more deliberate than a shaky screen recording anyway
- **Editing + captions:** CapCut (free, handles captions automatically)
